# Specification

What gerdTV does, per platform. Sections fill in as features are built; this is the current state.

## Platforms
| Platform | Status |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | main target; bootstrap app builds in CI |
| Android TV | second target; debug APK builds in CI |
| iPhone / iPad | third target; unsigned IPA from releases |
| Linux desktop (x64) | supported; the test platform |
| Android phone | ships with the Android build |

## Current behaviour
- **First run:** the provider's Xtream Codes login (server, username, password) or an M3U playlist URL, each with an optional User-Agent. Connecting runs the first sync; a refused login or an unreachable server is shown under the button and the form stays filled. The login goes to the platform's secure storage only.
- **Profiles:** one provider account each. Settings → Account switches to another, adds one (the setup form) and sets whether this one opens without asking; with several and none set, the app starts with a picker (the last one used first, "Always open this one"). Removing a profile opens another one, the picker, or setup after the last.
- **Start:** the saved account opens from its cached catalogue at once; a background sync refreshes it (a thin bar shows while it runs; a failed sync says so with Retry).
- **Browse:** TV (the channel lists or the guide grid, switched beside its search field and remembered per profile), Movies, Series: a Favourites group on top (when the section has any; in the section's item order), then the provider's groups with counts, then a group's items (paged, with the group's own search) in the section's list mode: compact list, list (logo, name (no channel number; sort and search still use it), what's on now with its progress, what's next), tiles or large tiles; chosen per section in Settings → Appearance → Lists or with the list bar's toggle (defaults: list for TV, tiles for movies and series); series open their page with the episodes (fetched when opened), movies their page; both pages show the panel's backdrop, the facts (year, length, rating, genre), plot, director and cast (Xtream's get_vod_info/get_series_info, asked when the page opens), Play on a movie, and Trailer where the panel names a YouTube trailer: after 2 s it plays muted behind the title (Android, iPhone/iPad, macOS, in YouTube's embedded player in a web view), Trailer plays it full screen with sound, Linux opens it in the browser. The star on a row or tile, a long press, or holding OK on the remote adds or removes a favourite; on a channel the long press or held OK opens its menu instead (Watch, add to or remove from favourites; in the guide a tap on the channel's name opens it too). Movies and Series start with Continue watching: movies and episodes left after 2 min and before 95 %, last watched first; they continue where they were left, from there or from their group. From 95 % a movie or episode is marked as watched (a check mark on rows, tiles and its page; its page or the episode's row marks and unmarks it). After an episode the next one of its series plays after a 10 s countdown with Play now and Cancel (Settings → Playback, on by default). Logos and posters are downloaded once and kept in the cache folder. **Sort:** a sort button above the groups (provider order, name A–Z or Z–A) and in each group's top bar (also channel number for Live TV; newest or oldest year, recently added and rating for movies and series, the last two only with Xtream Codes), remembered per section; favourites, zapping and the guide follow the items' order, and missing values go last.
- **Own groups and names:** holding a channel, movie or series opens its menu: watch, favourite, add to an own group (new or existing), and rename (channels); inside an own group also move and remove. Own groups sit above the provider's in lists and guide, keep their items' order (with the section sorted by provider order) and search the section. Holding a group renames it, moves it up, down or to the top, and deletes an own one. An empty name is the provider's again (a group's from the next update). The provider's groups keep their channels' order.
- **OpenSubtitles:** with the user's own API key (Settings → Account → OpenSubtitles, app-wide, in secure storage) the player's audio and subtitles sheet offers Find subtitles online for movies and episodes in gerdTV's player: by TMDB's id when known, else the cleaned title, in the preferred subtitle language (else the app's and English); a tap downloads it and shows it until the next start. Without a login a free key allows 5 downloads a day; an optional OpenSubtitles login (checked when saved) uses the account's quota.
- **TMDB:** with the user's own API key (Settings → Account → TMDB, app-wide, in secure storage), movie and series pages get from TMDB what the provider leaves empty (plot, genre, cast, director, release, length, rating, backdrop, trailer); M3U profiles get all of it. Matched by the panel's TMDB id, else by the cleaned title and year when that is unambiguous; kept 30 days. About shows TMDB's logo and notice.
- **Premiumize:** linked per profile in Settings → Account with the API key (premiumize.me → Account), or with a code typed at premiumize.me/device in builds that carry gerdTV's client id; a refused key shows Premiumize's reason. While linked, Premiumize is a section of its own after Series: its folders and files (playable ones by type or extension, with their size) and Transfers with their progress (a finished one opens its folder); files play like a recording, without Continue watching.
- **Watch tracking:** Trakt and Simkl, linked per profile in Settings → Account with a device code (trakt.tv/activate, simkl.com/pin); Trakt first needs the user's own Trakt app (client ID and secret, since no secret ships with gerdTV), Simkl uses gerdTV's client ID from the build. Movies and episodes are scrobbled as they play: start (also after a pause), pause, and stop with the progress when they end or another starts; both count them as watched from 80 %. Titles go without the provider's decorations ("DE - Inception (2010) 4K" as Inception, 2010); live TV, catch-up and Premiumize files aren't sent; a failed scrobble never interrupts playback. Scrobbles carry TMDB's id when gerdTV knows it. From Simkl, after each update (at most every 30 min) or with Import now in its settings: watched movies and episodes become watched marks (matched by TMDB id, else the cleaned title and year; an episode of a series not opened yet is marked once it is), and a paused position is taken where gerdTV knows the length; newer wins, nothing is removed. A mark set or removed by hand goes to Simkl's history.
- **Search:** TV's lists, TV's guide, Movies and Series each have a search field on top; typing a letter or digit anywhere in a section starts it, `/` or Ctrl/Cmd+F focuses it. On a section's group list it searches the whole section (Series: series and episodes); inside a group only that group, with an "All groups" chip that widens it to the section. Matches come in tiers: word starts (accents and case don't matter) or a channel number, words of the group's name ("dazn" finds every channel of a DAZN group), the middle of a word ("sport" finds Eurosport), typos (one from 4 letters, two from 7; the first letter must be right); within a tier favourites and recently watched come first, then names starting with the query, then provider order. Groups whose name matches are listed first and open with a tap; results keep the section's list mode and show each one's group with the matched text in bold. Live TV lists 5 matching programmes below the channels with Show all (running and upcoming soonest first, then under Earlier the ended ones, most recent first; up to 100 each); the Guide's field lists programmes instead of the grid. A line "Also in Movies 3 · Series 2" (counting word matches) switches to that section with the same query. Each section keeps its query until it is cleared; an empty, focused field lists the section's last 5 searches whose result was opened. Enter opens the top result (a series opens its episodes). On Android the empty field has a mic that starts the system's speech recognizer.
- **Player:** full screen with media_kit's controls; the watchdog recovers drops and stalls by itself and shows "Reconnecting (n)…"; a refused stream shows the reason instead of retrying; Space pauses, S or the Stop button (beside play/pause at the bottom left; with a remote both stay visible) stops; when the stream has more than one audio track or any subtitles, a button lists them (language, title, codec; subtitles also Off) and a tap switches; the same sheet moves audio and subtitles in 50 ms steps until the next start (not with Apple's player). Settings → Playback → Audio and subtitles sets the preferred languages (subtitles also Off; Apple's player follows the device's) and text subtitles' size and background. The tune button also sets the picture format (whole picture, zoom, stretch, 16:9, 4:3), remembered per channel, movie or episode; Settings → Playback → Deinterlace (off by default) smooths interlaced channels with mpv, whose decoder then copies its frames back; on Android TV, Match the TV's refresh rate (off by default) switches the display to a multiple of the stream's frame rate in the same resolution, until Stop. On touch screens (switch in Settings → Playback → Player gestures, on by default): in movies and episodes a sideways swipe or a double tap on the left or right third seeks, a vertical swipe on the right half sets the player's volume, and one on the left half the screen's brightness (Android: gerdTV's window only; iOS: the screen's, put back when the player closes or the app leaves).
- **Playback keeps going:** only Stop or starting another stream ends it. Leaving the player (Esc/Back) keeps the stream running in the now-playing bar below every screen (live picture, title, watchdog state, Stop); tapping the bar reopens the player without restarting the stream.
- **Picture-in-Picture and background (Android phone and TV):** leaving the app while something plays (not while it is paused, has ended or failed) shrinks it to the system's floating window; with the screen off or in another app the stream keeps playing (a "gerdTV" notification shows while it runs) until Stop.
- **Background and Picture-in-Picture (iPhone/iPad):** swiping to Home while something plays (not while it is paused, has ended or failed) floats it in Apple's floating window by itself (the player's PiP button does it too); the button closes the player, so the stream shows once (the bar shows the logo meanwhile; the window's restore button or tapping the bar reopens the player); returning to the app closes the window; tapping the window shows play/pause and skip; closing it keeps the sound until Stop, as does locking the screen; back in the app the picture continues without reconnecting. Movies and series (`.mkv` included) float too.
- **One player for everything (iPhone, iPad, Mac):** gerdTV's player plays live channels, movies and series in every mode (full screen, the bar, the floating window, background). Settings → Playback → Live TV player can choose Apple's player for live instead (after restarting gerdTV; a notice says so when the choice differs from what runs); gerdTV player version below it shows only with gerdTV's player and stays in use for movies: it plays gerdTV's own local stream, keeps the whole timeshift window, reconnects unseen, and floats in Apple's window on the Mac too; a channel it can't play goes to gerdTV's player.
- **Android TV home screen:** Play Next holds Continue watching (an entry removed there doesn't come back), and a gerdTV row the ten channels played last, both of the profile opened last; picking one plays it.
- **Lock screen (iPhone/iPad, Android; on the Mac the media keys and Control Center):** while something plays, the system's Now Playing card shows the title, the running programme of a channel, the logo or poster and play/pause (also from a headset and Control Center), with either player; movies and episodes show their progress, seeking from the card is off; Stop removes it.
- **Mini player (desktop):** the player's mini-player button (or P) shrinks the window to a floating, always-on-top picture (on every Space on macOS); a tap, mouse movement or Tab shows Mute, Restore and Stop for 3 s (a second tap hides them); drag it anywhere; playback continues. M mutes here and in the full player (desktop).
- **Buffer:** Settings → Playback chooses the profile's preset (Fast zap ~2 s, Balanced ~8 s, Smooth ~30 s, Max smooth ~90 s, Custom 1–300 s); the player's buffer button sets a channel's own preset (applied at once, "Use the default" goes back). Settings → Playback shows buffered seconds against the target in the player (off by default). Adaptive mode (opt-in): a channel that rebuffers more than 3 times in 10 minutes gets one step more and a short notice; never lowered automatically. The cache size follows the channel's measured bitrate.
- **Pause and rewind live:** live channels keep a back buffer (Settings → Playback: off, 30, 60 or 120 min; default 60 on desktop, 30 on phone/TV) on disk. The player's seek bar spans it up to live; while behind live the top bar shows how far ("−5:12") and Live, which jumps back to the live edge (twice the buffer's wait plus 1 s short of it, so the picture doesn't stall at once; that close counts as live); Left/Right skip 2 s on desktop, 10 s with the D-pad on phone and TV.
- **Updates:** Settings → About → Updates checks the latest release: only a correctly signed manifest (the app's compiled-in key), a newer version and a build for this platform are offered, with the release notes; Download keeps the file only when its size and SHA-256 match. Then macOS offers Install and restart (only a bundle that meets the installed app's code signing requirement replaces it; the app quits, is swapped and starts again), Android offers Install (the system's installer asks to confirm), Linux shows the file in its folder, and iPhone/iPad show a hint to re-sign the IPA. Every platform has a Release page link to the newest release's notes and files (where nothing can open it, the address shows).
- **Your data:** Settings → Account → Your data exports the open profile's favourites, recents, where movies and episodes were left, hidden groups, recent searches, reminders, manual guide mapping, settings, per-channel buffers and extra guide URLs as one JSON file without the login (macOS/Linux: a save dialog; Android: the Downloads folder; iPhone/iPad: the share sheet) and imports such a file with the system's file picker, merged: newer entries win, nothing is deleted.
- **Settings:** in five categories: Account, in subcategories: the provider (last sync, with Xtream Codes also when the account ends and how many streams it plays at once, the User-Agent sent to the provider (its API, guide and streams), profiles, update now, Your data, remove the account), Premiumize, Trakt and Simkl, Playback (buffer, adaptive mode, timeshift, player engine, on Apple live channels' player, on macOS the menu bar mode, Stream health: every recovery), TV guide, Appearance (theme: light, dark or the system's, eight accent colours, neutral or tinted surfaces, pure black; the player banner's translucency; logo, list modes, hidden groups: a ticked group disappears from the lists, search, the guide, favourites and continue watching), About (version, Updates, licences). A search field filters every setting as you type (each word must match a setting's name, its keywords or its category; accents don't matter) and shows the matches grouped by category.
- **Zapping in the player:** a live channel opened from a group or the guide switches with Up/Down to the previous/next channel of that list (wrapping); a banner shows the channel, what's on now with its progress and what's next for 5 s after each start or switch and on OK, at the bottom above the controls, white on black with the picture showing through (Settings → Appearance: 0–80 %, default 40 %, app-wide); CH+/CH− do the same on a remote, and its last-channel key, Backspace or the player's button returns to the channel before; OK opens the channel list beside the picture (what's on now per channel; pick one to switch), Esc closes it first. A channel opened from search switches through the search's channels.
- **Catch-up:** on channels whose provider keeps an archive (Xtream Codes `tv_archive`; M3U catch-up is not supported yet), a programme's sheet offers Watch for programmes that ended within the archive's days and From the start for the running one; it plays like a recording (seekable). The start minute follows the server's clock (its offset is read at every sync).
- **Recordings (not iPhone/iPad):** a programme's sheet records it (2 min before to 10 min after), the player's tune sheet records the channel now (to the running programme's end plus 10 min, 2 h without a guide). Recordings run while gerdTV runs (desktop also in the menu bar; Android with its background notification); on Android a planned start while gerdTV is closed shows a notification, and opening gerdTV starts it. They go into Settings → Playback → Recordings folder (desktop: any folder; Android: the system's folder picker, on TVs without one the app's folder on a drive), else Movies/gerdTV or the app's own storage, as MPEG-TS. Drops reconnect; a refused stream or a full disk ends it with the reason. When the account's streams are all in use, a notice comes a minute ahead with Skip, and at the start playback stops. The TV section lists them above the groups: play, stop, delete (with confirmation).
- **Programme sheet:** from the guide grid and from search: title, episode, channel and time, genre, description, and Watch (live, from the archive, or the channel); future programmes get Remind me.
- **Reminders:** a system notification 5 minutes before the start (Android: may be a few minutes late; permission asked with the first reminder); a tap plays the channel. Linux has no scheduled notifications: there the app shows them while it runs. Reminders are user data in the profile.
- **Guide grid:** TV's guide view shows one group's channels down the left and the day's programmes as cells along a local-time ruler (fixed column and ruler, a line at now, titles stick to the visible edge); the header shows the focused programme with its description (before anything is focused, the first row's running programme); a sort button (the order is shared with the lists); jumps to Now, 20:15 and any kept day; a cell opens the programme's sheet (title, episode, channel, time, genre, description) whose Watch plays the channel. Keyboard, remote and mouse move between cells.
- **TV guide:** the provider's own guide (Xtream Codes `xmltv.php`, or an M3U playlist's `url-tvg`) plus extra XMLTV URLs (plain or gzip), gzip or xz; refreshed after each sync and whenever a source is older than 12 h (Pluto TV and Samsung TV Plus: 6 h); kept 2 days back and 7 days ahead on desktop, 3 on phone/TV. Settings → Guide shows per source how many of the profile's channels it fills, its last import or error, adds and removes extra URLs or community guides (files of epgshare01, free-epg, Rytec, Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus and open-epg in one searchable list without the ones already added; a tap on a source shows its URL with Copy and its own time offset), sets the provider's time offset in whole hours (−12 to +12) and refreshes on demand. Programmes are stored only for channels the profile has. Matching: the manual mapping, the guide id, then the name without country code, quality tags and accents; a name from another country never matches (Germany, Austria and Switzerland share), the provider's guide wins where sources overlap, a schedule that has ended doesn't count, and a channel takes the guide of a matched one with the same name in another quality. Channels without a guide are listed (searchable), each with up to three suggestions that map with one tap or any guide channel to pick; mappings are user data, win over the automatic match and can be undone (Mapped by hand). Downloads time out, are limited in size, skip unchanged files, and a broken or empty file keeps the previous guide.
- English and German, following the system language; dark theme.

## Planned for v1 (built phase by phase)
Live TV, movies and series from Xtream Codes or M3U with provider groups; TMDB enrichment; search in every section that narrows per keystroke; EPG with a guide grid, programme search, reminders and recordings; a buffer the user chooses and a watchdog that recovers stalled streams without user action; timeshift and catch-up; mini player / Picture-in-Picture; multiple profiles; hidden groups; Google Cast and AirPlay; an in-app updater.

## Known platform limits
- iOS/iPadOS: no recording (the platform suspends background apps).
- iOS/iPadOS: updates are notified only; the IPA is re-signed and installed by hand.
- Linux: updates are downloaded and verified, then replaced by hand.
- macOS menu bar mode (Settings → Playback, on by default): closing the window, the Dock's Quit or Cmd-Q hide gerdTV into the menu bar (no Dock icon) and playback goes on; the menu bar item opens it, plays or pauses, stops, or quits; logout and shutdown quit at once.
- macOS: no app sandbox (the updater replaces the bundle in place); the first start without it copies the old container's data over and leaves the container as a backup.
- Player engine: Settings → Playback offers gerdTV's own libmpv build (newer mpv and FFmpeg) besides the standard one on Linux, Android, macOS, iPhone and iPad; the choice applies after a restart.
