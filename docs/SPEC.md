# Specification

What gerdTV does, per platform. Sections fill in as features are built; this is the current state.

## Platforms
| Platform | Status |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | main target; bootstrap app builds in CI |
| Android TV | second target; debug APK builds in CI |
| iPhone / iPad | third target; unsigned IPA from releases |
| Linux desktop (x64) | supported; the test platform |
| Android phone | ships with the Android build |

## Current behaviour
- **First run:** the provider's Xtream Codes login (server, username, password) or an M3U playlist URL. Connecting runs the first sync; a refused login or an unreachable server is shown under the button and the form stays filled. The login goes to the platform's secure storage only.
- **Profiles:** one provider account each. Settings → Account switches to another, adds one (the setup form) and sets whether this one opens without asking; with several and none set, the app starts with a picker (the last one used first, "Always open this one"). Removing a profile opens another one, the picker, or setup after the last.
- **Start:** the saved account opens from its cached catalogue at once; a background sync refreshes it (a thin bar shows while it runs; a failed sync says so with Retry).
- **Browse:** Live TV, Movies, Series: a Favourites group on top (when the section has any; in the section's item order), then the provider's groups with counts, then a group's items (paged, with the group's own search) in the section's list mode: compact list, list (logo, name (no channel number; sort and search still use it), what's on now with its progress, what's next), tiles or large tiles; chosen per section in Settings → Appearance → Lists or with the list bar's toggle (defaults: list for Live TV, tiles for movies and series); series open their page with the episodes (fetched when opened), movies their page; both pages show the panel's backdrop, the facts (year, length, rating, genre), plot, director and cast (Xtream's get_vod_info/get_series_info, asked when the page opens), Play on a movie, and Trailer where the panel names a YouTube trailer: after 2 s it plays muted behind the title (Android, iPhone/iPad, macOS, in YouTube's embedded player in a web view), Trailer plays it full screen with sound, Linux opens it in the browser. The star on a row or tile, a long press, or holding OK on the remote adds or removes a favourite. Movies and Series start with Continue watching: movies and episodes left after 2 min and before 95 %, last watched first; they continue where they were left, from there or from their group. Logos and posters are downloaded once and kept in the cache folder. **Sort:** a sort button above the groups (provider order, name A–Z or Z–A) and in each group's top bar (also channel number for Live TV; newest or oldest year, recently added and rating for movies and series, the last two only with Xtream Codes), remembered per section; favourites, zapping and the guide follow the items' order, and missing values go last.
- **Premiumize:** linked per profile in Settings → Account with the API key (premiumize.me → Account), or with a code typed at premiumize.me/device in builds that carry gerdTV's client id; a refused key shows Premiumize's reason. While linked, Movies starts with a Premiumize cloud row: its folders and files (playable ones by type or extension, with their size) and Transfers with their progress (a finished one opens its folder); files play like a recording, without Continue watching.
- **Search:** Live TV, Movies, Series and the Guide each have a search field on top; typing a letter or digit anywhere in a section starts it, `/` or Ctrl/Cmd+F focuses it. On a section's group list it searches the whole section (Series: series and episodes); inside a group only that group, with an "All groups" chip that widens it to the section. Matches come in tiers: word starts (accents and case don't matter) or a channel number, words of the group's name ("dazn" finds every channel of a DAZN group), the middle of a word ("sport" finds Eurosport), typos (one from 4 letters, two from 7; the first letter must be right); within a tier favourites and recently watched come first, then names starting with the query, then provider order. Groups whose name matches are listed first and open with a tap; results keep the section's list mode and show each one's group with the matched text in bold. Live TV lists 5 matching programmes below the channels with Show all (running and upcoming soonest first, then under Earlier the ended ones, most recent first; up to 100 each); the Guide's field lists programmes instead of the grid. A line "Also in Movies 3 · Series 2" (counting word matches) switches to that section with the same query. Each section keeps its query until it is cleared; an empty, focused field lists the section's last 5 searches whose result was opened. Enter opens the top result (a series opens its episodes). On Android the empty field has a mic that starts the system's speech recognizer.
- **Player:** full screen with media_kit's controls; the watchdog recovers drops and stalls by itself and shows "Reconnecting (n)…"; a refused stream shows the reason instead of retrying; Space pauses, S or the Stop button stops; when the stream has more than one audio track or any subtitles, a button lists them (language, title, codec; subtitles also Off) and a tap switches. On touch screens (switch in Settings → Playback → Player gestures, on by default): in movies and episodes a sideways swipe or a double tap on the left or right third seeks, a vertical swipe on the right half sets the player's volume, and one on the left half the screen's brightness (Android: gerdTV's window only; iOS: the screen's, put back when the player closes or the app leaves).
- **Playback keeps going:** only Stop or starting another stream ends it. Leaving the player (Esc/Back) keeps the stream running in the now-playing bar below every screen (live picture, title, watchdog state, Stop); tapping the bar reopens the player without restarting the stream.
- **Picture-in-Picture and background (Android phone and TV):** leaving the app while something plays shrinks it to the system's floating window; with the screen off or in another app the stream keeps playing (a "gerdTV" notification shows while it runs) until Stop.
- **Background and Picture-in-Picture (iPhone/iPad):** sound keeps playing with the screen locked or in another app; the player's PiP button hands the stream to Apple's player in a floating window (live channels and most VOD; `.mkv` movies continue in gerdTV instead), and closing PiP continues in gerdTV.
- **Mini player (desktop):** the player's mini-player button (or P) shrinks the window to a floating, always-on-top picture (on every Space on macOS) with Restore and Stop; drag it anywhere; playback continues.
- **Buffer:** Settings → Playback chooses the profile's preset (Fast zap ~2 s, Balanced ~8 s, Smooth ~30 s, Max smooth ~90 s, Custom 1–300 s); the player's buffer button sets a channel's own preset (applied at once, "Use the default" goes back). The player shows buffered seconds against the target. Adaptive mode (opt-in): a channel that rebuffers more than 3 times in 10 minutes gets one step more and a short notice; never lowered automatically. The cache size follows the channel's measured bitrate.
- **Pause and rewind live:** live channels keep a back buffer (Settings → Playback: off, 30, 60 or 120 min; default 60 on desktop, 30 on phone/TV) on disk. The player's seek bar spans it up to live; while behind live the top bar shows how far ("−5:12") and Live, which jumps back to the live edge; Left/Right skip 2 s on desktop, 10 s with the D-pad on phone and TV.
- **Updates:** Settings → About → Updates checks the latest release: only a correctly signed manifest (the app's compiled-in key), a newer version and a build for this platform are offered, with the release notes; Download keeps the file only when its size and SHA-256 match. Then macOS offers Install and restart (only a bundle that meets the installed app's code signing requirement replaces it; the app quits, is swapped and starts again), Android offers Install (the system's installer asks to confirm), Linux shows the file in its folder, and iPhone/iPad show a hint to re-sign the IPA. Every platform has a Release page link to the newest release's notes and files (where nothing can open it, the address shows).
- **Your data:** Settings → Account → Your data exports the open profile's favourites, recents, where movies and episodes were left, hidden groups, recent searches, reminders, manual guide mapping, settings, per-channel buffers and extra guide URLs as one JSON file without the login (macOS/Linux: a save dialog; Android: the Downloads folder; iPhone/iPad: the share sheet) and imports such a file with the system's file picker, merged: newer entries win, nothing is deleted.
- **Settings:** in five categories: Account (last sync, profiles, update now, Your data, Premiumize, remove the account), Playback (buffer, adaptive mode, timeshift, player engine, on macOS the menu bar mode, Stream health: every recovery), TV guide, Appearance (theme: light, dark or the system's, eight accent colours, neutral or tinted surfaces, pure black; logo, list modes, hidden groups: a ticked group disappears from the lists, search, the guide, favourites and continue watching), About (version, Updates, licences). A search field filters every setting as you type (each word must match a setting's name, its keywords or its category; accents don't matter) and shows the matches grouped by category.
- **Zapping in the player:** a live channel opened from a group or the guide switches with Up/Down to the previous/next channel of that list (wrapping); a banner shows the channel, what's on now with its progress and what's next for 5 s after each start or switch and on OK; OK opens the channel list beside the picture (what's on now per channel; pick one to switch), Esc closes it first. A channel opened from search switches through the search's channels.
- **Catch-up:** on channels whose provider keeps an archive (Xtream Codes `tv_archive`; M3U catch-up is not supported yet), a programme's sheet offers Watch for programmes that ended within the archive's days and From the start for the running one; it plays like a recording (seekable). The start minute follows the server's clock (its offset is read at every sync).
- **Programme sheet:** from the guide grid and from search: title, episode, channel and time, genre, description, and Watch (live, from the archive, or the channel); future programmes get Remind me.
- **Reminders:** a system notification 5 minutes before the start (Android: may be a few minutes late; permission asked with the first reminder); a tap plays the channel. Linux has no scheduled notifications: there the app shows them while it runs. Reminders are user data in the profile.
- **Guide grid:** the Guide section shows one group's channels down the left and the day's programmes as cells along a local-time ruler (fixed column and ruler, a line at now, titles stick to the visible edge); the header shows the focused programme with its description (before anything is focused, the first row's running programme); jumps to Now, 20:15 and any kept day; a cell opens the programme's sheet (title, episode, channel, time, genre, description) whose Watch plays the channel. Keyboard, remote and mouse move between cells.
- **TV guide:** the provider's own guide (Xtream Codes `xmltv.php`, or an M3U playlist's `url-tvg`) plus extra XMLTV URLs (plain or gzip), refreshed after each sync and whenever it is older than 12 h; kept 2 days back and 7 days ahead on desktop, 3 on phone/TV. Settings → Guide shows each source's last import or error, adds and removes extra URLs (or a community guide: a country's daily XMLTV file from the epgshare01 project, picked from a list and listed by its short name such as DE or US 2; a tap shows a source's URL with Copy), sets a time offset in whole hours (−12 to +12), refreshes on demand, and lists the channels without a guide (searchable by name; with several sources the provider's guide wins where they overlap; a provider channel without programmes counts as no guide, and a channel takes the guide of a matched one with the same name in another quality), each of which can be mapped to a guide channel by hand (the mapping is user data and wins over the automatic match).
- English and German, following the system language; dark theme.

## Planned for v1 (built phase by phase)
Live TV, movies and series from Xtream Codes or M3U with provider groups; TMDB enrichment; search in every section that narrows per keystroke; EPG with a guide grid, programme search, reminders and recordings; a buffer the user chooses and a watchdog that recovers stalled streams without user action; timeshift and catch-up; mini player / Picture-in-Picture; multiple profiles; hidden groups; Google Cast and AirPlay; an in-app updater.

## Known platform limits
- iOS/iPadOS: no recording (the platform suspends background apps) and no Picture-in-Picture (Apple's PiP needs AVPlayer, not mpv).
- iOS/iPadOS: updates are notified only; the IPA is re-signed and installed by hand.
- Linux: updates are downloaded and verified, then replaced by hand.
- macOS menu bar mode (Settings → Playback, on by default): closing the window, the Dock's Quit or Cmd-Q hide gerdTV into the menu bar (no Dock icon) and playback goes on; the menu bar item opens it, plays or pauses, stops, or quits; logout and shutdown quit at once.
- macOS: no app sandbox (the updater replaces the bundle in place); the first start without it copies the old container's data over and leaves the container as a backup.
- Player engine: Settings → Playback offers gerdTV's own libmpv build (newer mpv and FFmpeg) besides the standard one on Linux, Android, macOS, iPhone and iPad; the choice applies after a restart.
