# Design

The shell (`app/lib/screens/shell.dart`): a navigation rail on windows at least 720 px wide (desktop, tablet, TV), a bottom bar below that (phones). Sections: Live TV, Guide, Movies, Series, Settings; the first four have their search field on top (`app/lib/screens/section_search.dart`). Tokens: `app/lib/theme.dart` (accent, spacing unit 8, radius 12, motion 150 ms, the wide-layout breakpoint). The rules it follows:
- Form factors: desktop/tablet (left sidebar, the section's search on top), phone (bottom navigation), TV (10-foot layout, left menu, D-pad only, no touch-only controls).
- Calm and uncluttered: the platform's default font, a small type scale, generous spacing, one accent colour (orange, the logo's), dark (default) and light themes at WCAG AA contrast, visible focus rings, large hit targets. Posters and logos do the visual work.
- Keyboard and remote: Tab and the arrow keys reach every control, and the focused one is always scrolled into view from either side (`revealFocus` in `app/lib/main.dart`). Typing a letter or digit in a section starts its search; `/` and Ctrl/Cmd+F focus the field. In the player: Up/Down switch channels, OK (Enter/Select) opens the channel list, Left/Right seek, Space pauses, S stops, P mini player, Esc/Back leaves.
- Settings (`app/lib/screens/settings_screen.dart`): the search field on top; at 720 px and wider the categories sit in a 260 px column beside the chosen category, below that the categories are a list that opens a page per category; a search shows the matches grouped under their category titles.
- Logo (`app/lib/screens/app_logo.dart`): four mockups drawn in code, each carrying the name in stroked letters (the gTV wordmark, a TV screen with gTV, a g whose bowl is a TV, a g sending a signal) in the orange accent on a rounded `Tokens.ink` tile, picked in Settings → Appearance; it shows at the top of the rail, on the setup screen and in About, and is the app icon. `just logos` renders the launcher icons from the same painter.
- Every state is explicit: empty states say what to do next, loading uses skeleton rows, errors name what happened and offer one action. No modal dialogs except confirmations of destructive actions.
- Motion at most 150 ms, respecting the system's reduce-motion setting: every screen change is a 150 ms fade on every platform (`FadeTransitions` in `app/lib/theme.dart`), none with reduced motion. Bottom sheets still use their own slide.
- Design tokens live in `app/lib/theme.dart`; widgets use only tokens.
