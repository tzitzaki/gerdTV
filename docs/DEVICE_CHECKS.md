# Device checks

Things for the owner to look at on a real device; one line each, deleted once seen.

## Mac
- Own engine: Settings → Playback → Player engine gerdTV, restart; a channel and a movie play with picture and sound, hardware decoded.
- Look: posters fill movie tiles and lift under the pointer; Settings → Appearance → Theme switches light, accent and black at once.
- Search: typing in Live TV (no click) finds channels of every group and a show's programmes with their local time; Enter plays the top one.
- Movies and series: a movie plays (several languages: the subtitles button switches); a movie stopped after 5 min continues there.
- Favourites and hidden groups: a star adds a channel to Favourites atop Live TV; a group ticked in Settings → Hidden groups leaves lists and search.
- Keep playing: Esc keeps the channel in the bottom bar; closing the window keeps it in the menu bar (its menu: Open, Stop, Quit).
- Buffer: Settings → Playback → Smooth makes channels start later but play without stutters; the player shows "Buffer x / 30 s".
- Mini player: P shrinks to a floating picture that stays on top of other apps and on every Space; drag moves it; Restore returns.
- Catch-up: a past programme on a catch-up channel starts at its first minute (not an hour early or late), and seeking works.
- Guide: Settings → Guide shows the provider guide with channel and programme counts a minute after the first start.
- Zapping and timeshift: Up/Down switch channels; Space on live, then a minute later the top shows −1:00 and Live; the seek bar drags back.
- Guide grid: Now shows the running programmes under the now line; scrolling sideways keeps channel names and the time ruler aligned.
- Lists: a group shows logos, now with progress and next; the toggle cycles four modes; after a restart logos appear at once, even offline.
- Updates: Settings → About → Updates says up to date on the newest release; on an older one it offers the new version, downloads it and opens Downloads.
- Sandbox off: after updating from a sandboxed release, profiles, favourites and the login are still there without entering anything again.
- Install and restart: gerdTV quits and starts again on the new version within seconds; no `gerdTV.previous.app` is left in Applications.
- Logo: the Dock and Finder show the orange gTV icon; picking another logo in Settings → Appearance changes the Dock icon at once and after a restart.
- Your data: Export saves a JSON file where the dialog says; Import of that file on another Mac adds the favourites and reminders.

## iPhone and iPad
- Own engine: Player engine gerdTV, restart; a channel plays with picture and sound, also in PiP.
- Setup: the login connects and the catalogue appears; after closing and reopening the app it is still logged in.
- Live TV and a movie play in full screen with sound; logos show in the lists.
- Keep playing: Back from the player keeps the channel running in the bottom bar; Stop ends it.
- Background: locking the phone while a channel plays keeps the sound going; the lock screen shows it.
- PiP: the PiP button floats the channel over other apps; closing PiP continues in gerdTV (if it fails, note the message shown).
- Logo: picking another logo in Settings → Appearance shows iOS's "icon changed" alert and the home screen icon changes.
- Your data: Export opens the share sheet and "Save to Files" works; Import picks that file and reports the entries taken.
- iPad PiP: the player's PiP button floats a live channel over other apps (split view and home screen).
- Swipe: left edge closes a page; fast sideways switches section (not on Guide); player: seek, right half volume, left half brightness.

## Android phone
- Own engine: Player engine gerdTV, restart; a channel and a movie play smoothly (MediaCodec), and the picture-in-picture still works.
- PiP: Home while a channel plays shows it in a floating window; tapping it returns to the full player.
- Reminders: Remind me on a programme in 10 minutes asks for permission, notifies about 5 minutes before, and a tap plays it.
- Background: with the screen off the sound keeps playing for at least 10 minutes; the gerdTV notification opens the app.
- Logo: picking another logo in Settings → Appearance changes the launcher icon within a few seconds, and gerdTV keeps running.
- Voice search: the mic in Live TV's search field opens Google's speech prompt; the spoken words become the search.
- Swipe: left edge closes a page; fast sideways switches section (not on Guide); player: seek, right half volume, left half brightness.

## Shield
- Own engine: Player engine gerdTV, restart; HD channels play smoothly; the D-pad lifts tiles and reaches the sort button.
- Install: the APK installs and gerdTV appears in the TV launcher row with its banner.
- Remote: the D-pad reaches every section, list and the setup fields; OK opens; Back leaves the player.
- Live TV: a channel plays with picture and sound; hardware decoding keeps HD channels smooth.
- PiP: Home while a channel plays shows it in a corner window over the launcher; it keeps playing until Stop.
- Guide: the D-pad reaches every row of Settings → Guide, the offset list and the channels-without-guide screen.
- Plain HTTP: with a provider on http://, sync, logos, the guide and playback all still work (the cleartext config is gone).
- Lists: the D-pad moves through tiles row by row, the mode toggle is reachable; holding OK on a channel adds it to Favourites, a short OK plays.
- Search: Up reaches the section's search field; typed or spoken (its mic) words find channels of every group.
- Guide grid: the D-pad moves between programme cells in all four directions, the focused one stays in view, OK opens its sheet.
- Zapping and timeshift: Up/Down switch with the banner, OK opens the channel list; Left skips 10 s back ("−0:10", Live), Right forward to now.
- Updates: on an older release, Download then Install opens the system's installer (asking once to allow gerdTV) and the new version starts.
- Logo: picking another logo in Settings → Appearance changes the banner in the launcher row (if not, the banner stays the gTV wordmark).
- Your data: Export says Downloads/gerdtv-….json; Import opens a file picker that reaches that file (if none opens, note the error shown).
