# Device checks

Things for the owner to look at on a real device; one line each, deleted once seen.

## Mac
- Own engine: Settings → Playback → Player engine gerdTV, restart; a movie plays with picture and sound, hardware decoded.
- Look: posters fill movie tiles and lift under the pointer; Settings → Appearance → Theme switches light, accent and black at once.
- Search: typing in TV (no click) finds channels of every group and a show's programmes with their local time; Enter plays the top one.
- Movie page: plot and Play, after 2 s the trailer muted (Trailer: with sound); several languages: the subtitles button switches.
- Favourites and hidden groups: a star adds a channel to Favourites atop TV (hold: menu); a group ticked in Settings → Hidden groups leaves lists and search.
- Keep playing: Esc keeps the channel in the bottom bar; closing the window keeps it in the menu bar (its menu: Open, Stop, Quit).
- Buffer: Settings → Playback → Smooth makes channels start later but play without stutters; with the buffer shown (Playback): "Buffer x / 30 s".
- Mini player: P floats the picture on top on every Space; with another app in front hover or a click shows Mute, Restore, Stop; drag; M.
- Catch-up: a past programme on a catch-up channel starts at its first minute (not an hour early or late), and seeking works.
- Guide: Settings → Guide: each source shows your matched channels; an open-epg guide loads; suggestions map; Stop beside pause.
- Live (also Apple's player, Settings → Playback): zap, Space then −1:00 and Live, seek bar; Apple's: the PiP button floats it.
- Guide grid: Now shows the running programmes under the now line; scrolling sideways keeps channel names and the time ruler aligned.
- Lists: a group shows logos, now with progress and next; the toggle cycles four modes; after a restart logos appear at once, even offline.
- Updates: Settings → About → Updates says up to date on the newest release; on an older one it offers the new version, downloads it and opens Downloads.
- Sandbox off: after updating from a sandboxed release, profiles, favourites and the login are still there without entering anything again.
- Install and restart: gerdTV quits and starts again on the new version within seconds; no `gerdTV.previous.app` is left in Applications.
- Logo: the Dock and Finder show the orange gTV icon; picking another logo in Settings → Appearance changes the Dock icon at once and after a restart.
- Your data: Export saves a JSON file where the dialog says; Import of that file on another Mac adds the favourites and reminders.
- Media keys: with gerdTV playing, the keyboard's play/pause and Control Center pause and resume it, also with another app in front.
- Recordings: Record on a programme writes a .ts into Settings → Playback → Recordings folder; TV → Recordings plays and deletes it.
- Cast: tune sheet → Cast to a device lists the Shield; a channel and a movie play there; the bar's play/pause and Stop work.
- Deinterlacing on: an interlaced HD channel shows no combed edges and still plays smoothly; off again: as before.
- Subtitles online (with your OpenSubtitles key): Find subtitles online lists German ones for a movie; a tap shows them in the picture.

- Web player (host paired): a link from Settings → Web player opens in Safari and Chrome; a channel and a movie play, +3 h extends.
- Devices & sync: Show a code; on the Shield pick the Mac and type it; the Shield names the Mac first, Sync moves the login and favourites.

## iPhone and iPad
- Web player: the link's QR opens the page in Safari away from home; channels play; Add to Home Screen opens it full screen.
- Devices & sync: scanning the Mac's or Shield's pairing QR with the camera opens gerdTV, names the device, Sync moves the profile.
- Own engine: Player engine gerdTV, restart; a movie plays with picture and sound, also in PiP.
- Setup: the login connects and the catalogue appears; after closing and reopening the app it is still logged in.
- Live TV (also Das Erste HD: MP2 + AC-3 sound) and a movie play with sound; Back keeps the channel running in the bottom bar; Stop ends it.
- Home: floats only what plays, no black; returning closes the window (one picture); the button closes the player, restore reopens it.
- Lock: sound plays on 5 min, picture at once after; the lock screen card shows title, programme, logo; its and a headset's pause work.
- Logo: picking another logo in Settings → Appearance shows iOS's "icon changed" alert and the home screen icon changes.
- Your data: Export opens the share sheet and "Save to Files" works; Import picks that file and reports the entries taken.
- iPad: the floating window works over split view; live rewinds 20 min (timeshift 30 min in Settings → Playback) and Live returns.
- Trailer: a movie's page plays its trailer muted after 2 s; Trailer plays it with sound (if not, note YouTube's error number).
- Swipe: left edge closes a page; fast sideways switches section (not on Guide); player: seek, right half volume, left half brightness.
- Cast: the local network prompt comes once, then Cast to a device lists devices (Bonjour); casting a movie works.

## Android phone
- Own engine: Player engine gerdTV, restart; a channel and a movie play smoothly (MediaCodec), and the picture-in-picture still works.
- PiP: Home floats a playing channel (not after Stop or paused), a tap returns; lock screen: the card with title, programme, play/pause.
- Reminders: Remind me on a programme in 10 minutes asks for permission, notifies about 5 minutes before, and a tap plays it.
- Background: with the screen off the sound keeps playing for at least 10 minutes; the gerdTV notification opens the app.
- Logo: picking another logo in Settings → Appearance changes the launcher icon within a few seconds, and gerdTV keeps running.
- Voice search: the mic in TV's search field opens Google's speech prompt; the spoken words become the search.
- Swipe: left edge closes a page; fast sideways switches section (not on Guide); player: seek, right half volume, left half brightness.
- Cast: the local network prompt comes once, then Cast to a device lists the Shield; casting a channel works.
- Languages: Settings → Playback → Audio language German picks the German track of a two-language movie.

## Shield
- Own engine: Player engine gerdTV, restart; HD channels play smoothly; the D-pad lifts tiles and reaches the sort button.
- Install and remote: gerdTV is in the launcher row with its banner; the D-pad reaches every section and field; Back leaves the player.
- Movie page: OK on a movie opens its page with the muted trailer; OK plays, Trailer plays it with sound, Back returns.
- Live TV: a channel plays with picture and sound; hardware decoding keeps HD channels smooth.
- PiP: Home while a channel plays shows it in a corner window over the launcher; it keeps playing until Stop.
- Guide: the D-pad reaches every row of Settings → Guide, the offset list and the channels-without-guide screen.
- Plain HTTP: with a provider on http://, sync, logos, the guide and playback all still work (the cleartext config is gone).
- Lists: the D-pad moves through tiles row by row, the mode toggle is reachable; holding OK on a channel adds it to Favourites, a short OK plays.
- Search: Up reaches the section's search field; typed or spoken (its mic) words find channels of every group.
- Guide grid: the D-pad moves between programme cells in all four directions, the focused one stays in view, OK opens its sheet.
- Zapping: Up/Down, banner at the bottom; OK opens the list, Left from it Stop; Left skips 10 s back ("−0:10", Live), Right to now.
- Updates: on an older release, Download then Install opens the system's installer (asking once to allow gerdTV) and the new version starts.
- Logo: picking another logo in Settings → Appearance changes the banner in the launcher row (if not, the banner stays the gTV wordmark).
- Your data: Export says Downloads/gerdtv-….json; Import opens a file picker that reaches that file (if none opens, note the error shown).
- Recordings: a recording keeps running with the screen off; its folder: the system picker, or without one a USB drive's gerdTV folder.
- Recordings while closed: a planned one shows a notification at its start; opening gerdTV from it starts the recording.
- Cast: Cast to a device lists Chromecast devices (multicast); a channel plays there via the provider or gerdTV's own stream.
- Refresh rate on: a 50 fps channel switches the TV to 50 Hz (black for a moment), a 24 fps movie to 24 Hz, Stop goes back.
- Home screen: Play Next shows a movie left part-way and goes when you remove it there; the gerdTV row shows channels; OK plays.
- Remote: the last-channel key and Backspace go to the channel before and back; CH+ and CH− zap (if the remote has them).
- Own groups: holding a channel → Add to a group → New group; the group sits above the provider's, also in the guide; rename and move work.
- Next episode: an episode played to its end counts down 10 s to the next one; Cancel keeps it; the episode gets its check mark.
- Picture format: the tune sheet's Zoom fills a 4:3 channel's black bars; it stays for that channel after a restart.
