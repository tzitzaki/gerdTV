## libmpv, FFmpeg (Android)
gerdTV for Android bundles libmpv and FFmpeg as built by media_kit (media_kit_libs_android_video): mpv (2023, between 0.36 and 0.37) built with -Dgpl=false and FFmpeg 6.0, both under the GNU Lesser General Public License (FFmpeg: LGPL-3.0; mpv: LGPL-2.1 or later).

The build and its sources: https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-android-video-build (release v1.1.7). mpv: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv. FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org.

## libmpv, FFmpeg (macOS, iPhone, iPad)
gerdTV for macOS, iPhone and iPad bundles libmpv 0.36.0 and FFmpeg 6.0 as built by media_kit (media_kit_libs_macos_video, media_kit_libs_ios_video) in an LGPL configuration, under the GNU Lesser General Public License.

The build and its sources: https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-darwin-build (release v0.6.0). mpv: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv. FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org.

## libmpv (Linux)
gerdTV for Linux uses the libmpv installed by the system (media_kit_libs_linux); its licence is the one the distribution ships it under (see the libmpv package's copyright file).

## libmpv, FFmpeg, libplacebo (gerdTV build, Linux)
gerdTV for Linux also bundles its own libmpv (lib/libgerdmpv.so, Settings → Player engine): mpv 0.42.0 built with -Dgpl=false, FFmpeg 9.1 without --enable-gpl or --enable-nonfree, and libplacebo v7.387.0, linked into one library under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later, together with libdisplay-info 0.2.0 (MIT licence). System libraries it uses (libass, GnuTLS, VA-API, PulseAudio, ALSA, EGL/OpenGL) are not bundled.

The exact sources (pinned by SHA-256 or commit) and configure flags: scripts/build-libmpv.sh in the gerdTV repository, https://github.com/tzitzaki/gerdTV. mpv: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv. FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org. libplacebo: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libplacebo. libdisplay-info: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/emersion/libdisplay-info.

## libmpv, FFmpeg, libplacebo and text rendering (gerdTV build, Android)
gerdTV for Android also bundles its own libmpv (libgerdmpv.so, Settings → Player engine): mpv 0.42.0 built with -Dgpl=false and libplacebo v7.387.0 (GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later), FFmpeg 9.1 without --enable-gpl or --enable-nonfree and with --enable-version3 (GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later), mbedTLS 3.6.8 (Apache License 2.0), FreeType 2.14.3 (FreeType License), FriBidi 1.0.18 (GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later), HarfBuzz 15.2.0 (MIT licence) and libass 0.17.5 (ISC licence), linked into one library.

The exact sources (pinned by SHA-256 or commit) and configure flags: scripts/build-libmpv-android.sh in the gerdTV repository, https://github.com/tzitzaki/gerdTV. mbedTLS: https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls. FreeType: https://freetype.org. FriBidi: https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi. HarfBuzz: https://github.com/harfbuzz/harfbuzz. libass: https://github.com/libass/libass.
